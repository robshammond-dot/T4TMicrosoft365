Microsoft 365 Basics

Simple tips for the Microsoft tools you use every day: Outlook, Teams, OneDrive and our Knowledge Hub.

💻

Four tools, one sign-in. Your work email, team chat, files and this Knowledge Hub all sit in Microsoft 365, and you sign in to all of them with your work email address.

You don't need to know everything. These are the few things that make the biggest difference day to day.

📧 OutlookYour work email and calendar.
💬 TeamsChats, calls and meetings with the team.
☁️ OneDriveYour work files, saved safely and backed up.
📚 Knowledge HubAll your training and guides, built on SharePoint.

Every day

Email, Chat and Files

1

Outlook

  • Search instead of scrolling. Type a client's name, surname or booking reference into the search bar at the top to find every email about them.
  • Flag emails you need to come back to, so nothing slips through on a busy day.
  • Set an automatic reply when you're away, saying when you're back and who to contact in the meantime, so clients and colleagues aren't left waiting.
  • Keep your email signature up to date with your name, role and contact number.
💡
Can't find a setting? In Outlook, open Settings and type what you're looking for, for example "automatic replies" or "signature".
2

Teams

  • Use chat for quick questions to a colleague, instead of a long email.
  • Type @ and a name to get someone's attention in a group chat. They'll get a notification.
  • Set your status to Busy or Do not disturb when you're on a call with a client, so the team knows not to interrupt.
  • Join meetings from the link in your calendar invite. Check your camera and microphone on the screen before you join.
  • Share your screen in a meeting when you need help with something, so the other person sees exactly what you see.
3

OneDrive

  • Save work files to OneDrive, not your desktop. They're backed up and you can open them on any work device.
  • Share a link instead of an attachment when sending a file to a colleague, so everyone works on the same version.
  • Saved over something by mistake? Right-click the file and choose Version history to go back to an earlier copy.
  • Deleted a file? Check the Recycle bin in OneDrive. Deleted files stay there for a while before they're gone for good.
4

Our Knowledge Hub

  • Use the search box at the top of the Hub to find a guide, a policy or a destination quickly.
  • Click the star (Add to favourites) on the Hub so it's always one click away in SharePoint.
  • Start at Knowledge Hub Home if you're not sure where something lives. Every section is linked from there.

Staying Secure

5

Keep Our Clients' Information Safe

Never share your password, including with colleagues.
Complete your monthly IT security course when it's sent to you.
Lock your screen when you step away from your desk (Windows key + L).
Think before you click. If an email asks you to sign in, pay something or open an attachment you weren't expecting, check with your manager first.
⚠️
Clicked on something that didn't feel right? Tell your manager straight away. Speaking up quickly is always the right thing to do.
3
Handy

One Tip That Works Everywhere

✓

Find Any Setting

Outlook, Teams and OneDrive all have a search or settings search. If you're not sure where something is, type what you want to do, for example "out of office", "signature" or "notifications", and it will take you there.
